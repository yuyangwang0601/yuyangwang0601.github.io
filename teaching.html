Teaching

I have served as a Teaching Assistant at the University of Toronto Scarborough across multiple terms, primarily in undergraduate mathematics courses.

Responsibility Types

Type A

  1. Led weekly tutorials and explained core mathematical concepts.
  2. Held office hours and provided individualized academic support.
  3. Graded assignments, quizzes, and examinations using course rubrics.
  4. Invigilated tests and examinations.
  5. Coordinated with instructors and other teaching assistants on course coverage and grading standards.

Type B

  • Type A but without the duty of holding office hours and delivering tutorials.

Philosophy

I prefer to teach a general approach to mathematical problem solving rather than focusing only on how to solve a single problem. Rather than giving students direct answers, I try to break difficult problems into smaller steps and guide them through the reasoning process. My goal is to help students become more confident in approaching unfamiliar problems and develop independent mathematical thinking.
