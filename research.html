Current Project

Polynomial Stability of Sparse Matrix Patterns

Mentor: Prof. Michael Cavers

Status: In progress

  • Extending graph-theoretic methods for identifying potentially stable sparse matrix patterns from the 4 × 4 case to the 5 × 5 case.
  • Developing computational tools to enumerate strongly connected digraphs and filter candidates using Routh–Hurwitz conditions and linear-subgraph criteria.
  • Investigating sufficient stability certificates through combinatorial matrix theory and Lyapunov-based methods.
