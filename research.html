Research

My current interests include Galois theory, representation theory, numerical analysis, and financial mathematics, with particular interest in problems involving market microstructure noise.

Current Project

Polynomial Stability of Sparse Matrix Patterns

Mentor: Prof. Michael Cavers

Status: In progress

  • Extending graph-theoretic methods for identifying potentially stable sparse matrix patterns from the 4 × 4 case to the 5 × 5 case.
  • Developing computational tools to enumerate strongly connected digraphs and filter candidates using Routh–Hurwitz conditions and linear-subgraph criteria.
  • Investigating sufficient stability certificates through combinatorial matrix theory and Lyapunov-based methods.

Numerical Analysis Projects

MATD10 — Topics in Mathematics: Numerical Analysis

  • Project 1: Implemented and compared multiple finite-difference methods through convergence, stability, and CFL-condition experiments.
  • Project 2: Studied boundary treatments for finite-difference schemes, including ghost cells, one-sided differences, time-dependent boundary conditions, and RK4 time integration.
  • Project 3: Investigated SBP-SAT methods, energy stability, convergence behaviour, and numerical effects such as roundoff error.
